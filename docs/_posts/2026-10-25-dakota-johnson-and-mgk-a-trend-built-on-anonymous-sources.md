"Dakota Johnson" started climbing US Google Search late on Friday, October 24, 2026, past 5,000 searches. The trigger wasn't a movie, an award, or anything she said. It was a report, sourced to an unnamed insider, about how Megan Fox feels about Johnson dating Fox's ex-fiancé, Machine Gun Kelly.

That's the whole engine. Three people, none of whom have said anything on the record, and a week of photographs.

## What's Happening

According to a Just Jared write-up of a Page Six report published Friday, October 23, Megan Fox, 40, is said to be relieved that Machine Gun Kelly — real name Colson Baker, 36 — is pursuing a new relationship, with a source saying she has no interest in getting back together. The quoted line: "Megan isn't just unbothered by MGK dating Dakota — she's actually relieved he's moving on."

TMZ reported on October 22, citing sources familiar with the situation, that Fox is unbothered by the pairing and has no interest in getting involved in his love life. The same reporting said the two have no relationship outside co-parenting their 1-year-old daughter.

On the couple itself, People — as summarized by Just Jared on October 19 — had a source saying Johnson and MGK bonded over a shared dry sense of humor and are in a "dating phase" without being ready to go official.

HOLA reported that the two were photographed arriving at Taylor Swift's Tribeca apartment in September, and that per reports they hit it off after attending Swift and Travis Kelce's wedding in July.

Nobody involved has confirmed a relationship publicly.

## Why People Are Searching For It

The spike isn't really about Johnson. It's about the triangle.

Fox and MGK were one of the most-covered couples of the early 2020s, and a story that lets readers check in on all three at once outperforms a story about any one of them. The Friday night timing matters too — the "relieved" line landed, got aggregated within hours by half a dozen outlets, and pushed casual readers to search the newest name in the sentence.

Who's looking: people who followed the Fox–MGK saga and want the postscript, Johnson's film audience catching up, and a large group who saw a push notification and typed the name to figure out what they'd missed.

## The Context Most Coverage Skips

Here's the part the headlines compress into nothing: almost every claim in this cycle comes from an unnamed source, and the outlets differ on what the sources are even describing.

Run the timeline. MGK met Fox in 2020 on the set of "Midnight in the Switchgrass," and they announced their engagement in January 2022. Their relationship ended in late 2024, before the March 2025 birth of their daughter. On Johnson's side, Wikipedia lists her partnership with Chris Martin as running 2017 to 2025. Johnson and MGK reportedly crossed paths at the Swift–Kelce wedding, and were later seen in New York and in Milan. Johnson's split from Martin preceded the new pairing.

So the structure is: two high-profile breakups, a mutual-friend wedding, a September photograph, and six weeks of sightings. That is a thin factual spine carrying an enormous amount of narrative weight.

Now look at how the claims diverge. People's source describes a relationship with no label and unphotographed dates. In Touch's source frames Johnson as keeping things casual and quotes an insider saying he "loves that he's dating Hollywood royalty." The Daily Mail's sources went considerably further, offering intimate detail about the relationship. These are not the same story. They're three different stories wearing the same headline.

That's the pattern worth noticing. Confirmed-by-photograph events — two people leaving a studio, a car parked on a street — get wrapped in sourced emotional interpretation, and the interpretation is what travels. The photos are evidence. The feelings attributed to people who didn't speak are not.

## What We Know and What We Don't

Reasonably solid:

- Johnson and MGK have been photographed together repeatedly since September, including arriving at Taylor Swift's New York apartment and leaving an LA recording studio this month.
- Fox and MGK share a 1-year-old daughter and co-parent.
- Multiple major outlets ran source-based reports this week about Fox's reaction.

Unconfirmed:

- The relationship's status. Neither Johnson nor MGK has publicly confirmed it.
- Fox's actual feelings. Every version of that runs through an anonymous intermediary.
- The claim that MGK kept seeking Fox's attention post-breakup, including a flirtatious Instagram comment in March, comes from the same unnamed Page Six source — I could not independently verify the characterization.
- Representatives for the three reportedly did not immediately respond to requests for comment.

Also worth flagging: several pages circulating this story are low-quality aggregators rewriting Page Six and TMZ without adding reporting. If you see a fourth-hand version, it's the same two sources underneath.

## My Take

My read: this trend is a story about publishing incentives, not about three people's lives. A photograph of two adults leaving a building generates no clicks on its own. Attach a third person's reported emotional state to it and you get a week of coverage across dozens of outlets, all resting on sources nobody can evaluate.

I think the useful skill here is reading the attribution line before the headline. When a piece says "a source tells," you are being handed one unnamed person's framing, filtered through a reporter who may have good reason to trust them and no way to show you why. A source saying the two are in a "dating phase" but not ready to go official is a statement about what one person claims, and the honest version of that sentence is longer and duller than the headline.

The fair point on the other side: anonymous sourcing isn't inherently junk. Entertainment reporters at established outlets do develop real contacts, and the September and October photographs are genuine documentation that something is going on. Page Six and TMZ have broken accurate celebrity news for years. I'm not saying this is fabricated — I'm saying the confidence level in the coverage exceeds the confidence level the evidence supports.

What I'd watch for: an on-record statement, a joint public appearance, or a verified post from one of them. Until one of those arrives, every update is a new insider describing the same handful of photos. If you're reading this story for information rather than entertainment, there is very little new information per article, and the ratio keeps getting worse as more aggregators pile on.

And the thing I'd avoid entirely is treating any of it as insight into how these people actually feel. I don't know. The outlets don't either. The only honest position on a stranger's relationship is that the photographs show where someone's car was parked, and nothing more.
